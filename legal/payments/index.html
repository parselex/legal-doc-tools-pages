Recurring Payments & Subscription Terms

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This page explains how recurring billing works for paid Parselex plans and subscriptions offered through our applications. It is a summary of the billing terms that apply in addition to the plan conditions shown at checkout.

1. Automatic renewal

Paid plans renew automatically at the end of each billing period (monthly or annual, as selected) until cancelled. By starting a paid subscription you authorise us (via our payment processor) to charge the recurring fee to your chosen payment method at the start of each billing period.

2. Pricing and taxes

The price and billing period are displayed at checkout before you confirm. Prices may be exclusive or inclusive of taxes as stated at checkout; applicable taxes are calculated and shown before payment.

3. Payment processing

Payments are processed by third-party payment providers. Card details are collected and stored by the processor under PCI DSS controls; we do not store full card numbers on our systems. You may update or replace your payment method via your account or by contacting contact@parselex.app.

4. Cancellation

You may cancel at any time before the next renewal date — in your account settings or by emailing contact@parselex.app. Cancellation stops all future charges; your plan remains active until the end of the period you already paid for. No partial-period refunds are due for unused time unless required by law or stated at purchase.

5. Refunds

Refund conditions, if any, are stated at the point of purchase. Mandatory consumer-protection rights in your jurisdiction (including EU/UK withdrawal or Indian consumer law rights, as applicable) are not limited by this page.

6. Price changes

If a subscription price changes, we give you advance notice before the next renewal; you may cancel before the change takes effect to avoid the new price.

7. Failed payments

If a charge fails we may retry it and will contact you at your account email. Accounts may be suspended after a grace period if payment cannot be taken.

8. Contact

Billing questions: contact@parselex.app.
