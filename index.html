Security by Architecture, Not by Promise

Parselex runs on a Zero-Trust mesh network designed for the ethical and compliance requirements of legal practice. Access is brokered by LexConnect — our secure network client — and documents are shielded by edge-side PII redaction.

LexConnect — Zero-Trust Network Access

Zero Public Endpoints. Zero Attack Surface.

Workspaces do not exist on the public internet. The LexConnect client establishes an encrypted mesh tunnel with device verification and least-privilege access — inference nodes carry no public IP addresses.

The Ethics Firewall

Edge-Side PII Redaction

Names, identifiers and account numbers are replaced with deterministic tokens on your device before transmission. Our engines process the legal logic, not the personal identities — re-identification happens locally on return.

Data Sovereignty

Ephemeral Processing. No Content Logging.

Your case files never train public models. Inference runs in ephemeral sessions, and access is audited — who connected and when — while the payload itself is never logged.
